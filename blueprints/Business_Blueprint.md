# Trusted Discovery

PinkCurve is more than a commercial marketplace.

PinkCurve is a **trusted discovery platform** designed to help people find offerings and information relevant to their needs, interests, context, and communities.

A meaningful discovery does not always result in a purchase. It may help someone learn, attend, register, visit, contact, participate, save, share, or simply become aware of something useful.

PinkCurve therefore recognizes two broad forms of discovery:

## Commercial Discovery

Commercial discovery connects buyers with sellers offering products, services, experiences, promotions, subscriptions, events, and other commercial opportunities.

Examples include:

* Products
* Professional services
* Restaurants
* Travel experiences
* Classes
* Entertainment
* Promotions
* Discounts
* New offerings
* Local businesses

When PinkCurve creates measurable commercial value by connecting an interested buyer with a seller, that interaction may contribute to PinkCurve's revenue.

## Public and Community Discovery

PinkCurve may also help people discover useful information that does not have a commercial purpose.

Examples include:

* Public announcements
* Community events
* Public services
* Volunteer opportunities
* Charity activities
* Educational opportunities
* Local programs
* Community resources
* Public-interest information

These discoveries may generate no direct revenue for PinkCurve.

Their value is different.

They make PinkCurve more useful to people and communities and provide buyers with reasons to return even when they are not shopping.

# The Offering Model

PinkCurve uses **Offering** as a broad concept representing something useful for a buyer to discover.

An Offering may represent:

* A product
* A service
* An event
* An experience
* A promotion
* A digital offering
* An opportunity
* Public information
* A community announcement

An Offering does not require a transaction.

An Offering does not require a seller landing page.

The purpose of an Offering is to represent something potentially useful or relevant to a buyer.

---

# The Action Model

Every Offering may provide one or more appropriate actions.

Examples include:

* Learn More
* Visit Website
* View Product
* Contact Seller
* Request Quote
* Register
* Reserve
* Get Directions
* Call
* Save
* Share
* Attend
* Buy

Some offerings may require no action beyond informing the buyer.

The action should reflect the nature and purpose of the Offering rather than forcing every discovery into a commercial transaction.

---

# Commercial and Non-Commercial Interactions

PinkCurve distinguishes between **buyer interaction** and **billable interaction**.

A buyer interaction may include:

* Viewing
* Saving
* Sharing
* Learning more
* Requesting directions
* Registering
* Contacting
* Visiting a seller
* Purchasing through an external seller destination

Not every interaction should generate revenue.

PinkCurve should charge sellers only when appropriate commercial value is created under clearly defined and transparent pricing rules.

Public-service and community interactions should not accidentally become billable commercial events.

---

# Buyer Value

PinkCurve should give people reasons to visit even when they do not intend to purchase something.

A buyer should be able to open PinkCurve to:

* Find something they already need
* Discover something interesting
* See what is new
* Find relevant discounts
* Explore trends
* Discover nearby activities
* Learn about community events
* Find useful public information

PinkCurve's goal is not to maximize the amount of time people spend scrolling.

Its goal is to make discovery **useful, relevant, trustworthy, and enjoyable**.

A successful PinkCurve visit should frequently leave the buyer thinking:

**"I am glad I discovered that."**

---

# Relevance Before Revenue

PinkCurve's recommendations should primarily serve the interests of the buyer.

Commercial relationships must not secretly override relevance.

Paid promotion, when offered, should be clearly identified and should remain subject to PinkCurve's standards for trust, safety, and relevance.

PinkCurve should never intentionally make a less useful recommendation merely because it generates more revenue.

This principle protects the long-term relationship between PinkCurve and its buyers.

**Trust is more valuable than a single click.**

# The Buyer Value Flywheel

PinkCurve's business model depends on creating a healthy relationship between public value, buyer participation, seller value, and sustainable revenue.

```text
Useful Information
        +
Public & Community Discovery
        +
Commercial Offerings
        +
Enjoyable Discovery
        ↓
More Buyer Value
        ↓
More Buyer Participation
        ↓
Better Understanding of Intent
        ↓
Better Matching
        ↓
More Qualified Seller Connections
        ↓
Greater Seller Value
        ↓
Sustainable PinkCurve Revenue
        ↓
Better Platform
        ↓
More Buyer Value
        ↺
```

PinkCurve therefore recognizes that some activities that generate no direct revenue may still create significant long-term business value.

---

# Buyer Magnetism

Buyer participation is fundamental to PinkCurve.

Without buyers, sellers receive little value from participating in the platform.

PinkCurve must therefore continually answer four questions:

**Why does a buyer come?**

Because PinkCurve helps them find something useful.

**Why does a buyer stay?**

Because discovery is relevant, trustworthy, and enjoyable.

**Why does a buyer return?**

Because PinkCurve continually provides new value through offerings, trends, opportunities, community information, and personalized discovery.

**Why does a buyer recommend PinkCurve?**

Because PinkCurve consistently helps them discover things worth knowing about.

Buyer Magnetism is therefore a core business requirement rather than simply a marketing objective.

---

# Sustainable Revenue

Buyer access to PinkCurve remains free.

PinkCurve's commercial model should primarily generate revenue by creating measurable value for sellers.

Initially, this may include charges associated with qualified commercial traffic directed toward seller destinations.

Over time, additional revenue models may be considered where they clearly create value, including advanced seller intelligence, analytics, premium marketing capabilities, and other seller services.

PinkCurve should not pursue revenue mechanisms that materially undermine buyer trust.

The long-term objective is not to maximize revenue from every interaction.

The objective is to create a sustainable ecosystem in which:

* Buyers receive continuous value at no cost.
* Sellers receive measurable commercial value.
* Employees can build meaningful and sustainable careers.
* PinkCurve earns sufficient revenue to operate, innovate, protect trust, and grow responsibly.

---

# Business Principle

**PinkCurve thrives only when buyers, sellers, employees, and PinkCurve itself can thrive together.**

Commercial success and public value are not opposing objectives.

When properly designed, useful public discovery attracts buyers, buyer participation creates opportunities for sellers, seller success supports PinkCurve, and a financially healthy PinkCurve can continue investing in buyers, sellers, employees, communities, and trust.

---

# Strategic Definition

PinkCurve is in the business of **trusted discovery**.

It helps people discover what matters to them—whether the outcome is commercial, informational, or community-oriented.

PinkCurve earns its living when trusted discovery creates measurable commercial value for sellers.

**Technology evolves. Trust endures.**

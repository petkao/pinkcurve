# PinkCurve Blueprints

The **Blueprints** define what PinkCurve is building and why.

They bridge the gap between the company's Constitution and the technical implementation. While the Constitution establishes PinkCurve's philosophy and guiding principles, the Blueprints translate those principles into products, capabilities, and user experiences.

Blueprints are intentionally **technology-independent**. They focus on business strategy, customer value, trust, and long-term product direction rather than programming languages, frameworks, or infrastructure.

## Relationship to the Repository

```
Constitution
      ↓
Blueprints
      ↓
Architecture
      ↓
Implementation
```

* **Constitution** — Defines PinkCurve's mission, vision, values, and principles.
* **Blueprints** — Describe the products, experiences, and capabilities PinkCurve will create.
* **Architecture** — Describes how those capabilities will be implemented technically.
* **Implementation** — Source code, services, AI components, and deployment.

## Guiding Principles

Every blueprint should:

* Align with the PinkCurve Constitution.
* Create measurable value for buyers and sellers.
* Strengthen trust and transparency.
* Be understandable by both business and engineering teams.
* Remain stable even as technologies evolve.

## Living Documents

These blueprints are living documents. They will evolve as PinkCurve grows, while remaining faithful to the company's Constitution and long-term vision.
